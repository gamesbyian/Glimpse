Eight product-owner decisions · deliberately unresolved
D01 · Home photographic crescent

Keep a clearly labeled, tap-only crescent collection cover or use a conventional card?

D02 · Finite photographic reel

Do physical five-aperture paper rim, zoom, pointer, hero and clipped wheel communicate a usable photo selector?

D03 · Action hierarchy

At normal and 200% text, do Grid, Next/Previous, Set wallpaper and external sharing appear understandable and sufficiently accessible?

D04 · Selected vs applied

Is app-acknowledged Home p04 clearly distinguished from selected p12 and unknown current system wallpaper?

D05 · Provider lifetime

Are locally verified images and pending Google Photos source items correctly represented under offline/revoked/low-storage conditions?

D06 · OS-owned UI and background claims

Are system picker/sharesheet/Lock availability and best-effort five-minute scheduling portrayed without inventing guaranteed results?

D07 · Restore consequences

Are preview, Skip/Keep/Separate, committed outcome and Back clearly separate and safely reversible?

D08 · Whole-product visual language

Do support, recovery, accessibility and native entry points feel like the same Glimpse product as the photographic paper reel?
